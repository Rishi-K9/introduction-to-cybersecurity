**1.Introduction to Cybersecurity**

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, devices, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft. It helps individuals and organizations keep their information safe and maintain secure digital systems.

**2. Purpose of Cybersecurity**

1. Protect Data - Personal and organization information from unauthorized access.
2. Prevent Cyber Attacks - Prevent malware, phishing, ransomware, and other cyber attacks.
3. Protect Privacy - Keep users personal information secure and private.
4. Maintain System Security - Keep computers, servers, and networks secure.
5. Reduce Financial Loss - Reduce financial losses caused by cyber attacks.

**3. CIA Triad**

The CIA Triad is a fundamental model in cybersecurity that consists of three principles:

- Confidentiality - Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized people.
- Integrity - Ensures that information remains accurate, complete, and is not changed without authorization.
- Availability - 	Ensures that information and systems are accessible to authorized users whenever needed.

**4. Personal vs Organizational Security Risks** 

Personal Security Risks
- Phishing emails
- Password theft
- Identity theft
- Malware
- Social engineering


Organizational Security Risks
- Data breaches
- Ransomware
- Network attacks
- Insider threats
- Service disruption

**Personal Security Risks -** A threat to an individual’s device, account, or personal information.

**Organizational Security Risk -** A threat to a company’s systems, networks, applications, or business data.

**5. Importance of Cybersecurity**

- Protects sensitive information
- Prevents unauthorized access
- Protects user privacy
- Reduce financial losses
- Maintains business operation
- Protects organizations from cyber attacks
- Builds customer trust
- Helps maintain secure networks

**6. 10 Common Cyber Threats**

1. Phishing
  
   Fake emails or messages used to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

2. Malware
  
   Malicious software that can damage devices or steal information.

3. Ransomware
  
   Malware that locks or encrypts files and prevents access.

4. Password Attacks
  
   Attempts to steal or crack passwords to gain unauthorized access.

5. Social Engineering
  
   Tricking people into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

6. Identity Theft
  
   Stealing and misusing someone’s personal information.

7. Data Breach
  
   Unauthorized access to or exposure of sensitive information.

8. DoS Attack
  
   An attack that makes a website or service unavailable to users.

9. Insider Threat
  
   Misuse of authorized access by an employee or trusted person.

10. Man-In-The-Middle (MitM) Attack
  
    An attack where communication between two parties is secretly intercepted.

**Conclusion**

Cybersecurity is important for protecting data, devices, networks, and systems from cyber threats. Understanding the CIA Triad and common threats helps individuals and organizations improve their security and reduce cyber risks.
